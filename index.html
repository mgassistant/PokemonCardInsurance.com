Compare protection

Homeowners coverage and specialty collectibles coverage are not always the same.

The right choice depends on your existing policy and the specialty policy offered. Always compare actual terms, limits, deductibles, exclusions and valuation provisions.

Standard homeowners / renters

  • May contain limits for certain categories of property
  • May use deductibles that are significant relative to the loss
  • Valuation method may not match collectible-market expectations
  • Transit, shows or business inventory may need separate review

Specialty collectibles policy

  • Designed specifically around collectible property
  • May offer scheduled or blanket collection approaches
  • Can consider documentation, current values and collection use
  • May provide options tailored to collectors, subject to policy terms
Collector resource

Pokémon card insurance for modern collections.

High-value Pokémon collections can include professionally graded cards, raw singles, sealed booster boxes, elite trainer boxes, vintage products, promotional cards, and complete sets. As collection values grow, collectors often want to understand whether their homeowners or renters policy is enough—or whether a specialty collectibles insurance policy may be a better fit.

PokemonCardInsurance.com is designed to help collectors organize the information an insurance professional may need to review a collection. That can include estimated total value, highest-value items, grading certification details, purchase records, storage methods, shipping activity, card-show attendance, and whether the collection is personal or used as business inventory.

Insurance for graded Pokémon cards

Graded cards can be easier to document because they typically have a grading company, grade, certification number, and comparable market data. Depending on the insurer and collection value, an underwriter may still request photographs, receipts, inventory reports, appraisals, or additional verification.

Insurance for sealed Pokémon products

Sealed collections can include booster boxes, elite trainer boxes, collection boxes, cases, tins, and other unopened products. If you want sealed inventory included in a quote request, document quantities, product names, acquisition costs when available, and current estimated values.

When should a collector request a specialty insurance review?

A review may be worth considering when a collection becomes difficult to replace out of pocket, when individual items have significant value, when cards are frequently shipped or sent for grading, when collections travel to shows, or when the collector is unsure how their existing homeowners or renters policy would respond to a loss.

Can Pokémon cards be insured?

Specialty collectibles policies may be available for eligible trading card collections. Availability, limits, exclusions, valuation requirements and covered causes of loss depend on the carrier and policy.

Can graded Pokémon cards be insured?

Eligible graded cards may be insurable. The insurer may request certification numbers, photos, receipts, current values or appraisals depending on the item and total collection value.

Can sealed Pokémon products be insured?

Some programs may consider sealed products such as booster boxes and elite trainer boxes. Underwriting requirements vary, especially for high-value or business inventory.

Does homeowners insurance cover a Pokémon card collection?

It can provide some coverage, but policy limits, deductibles, exclusions and valuation methods may differ from specialty collectibles coverage. Review the actual policy rather than assuming the collection is fully protected.

Do I need an appraisal?

Not every collection requires the same documentation. The carrier may ask for an appraisal or additional proof of value depending on collection size, individual item values, and underwriting requirements.
